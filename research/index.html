About

Conall Murray is a theoretical physicist and originator of Unified Delay Relativity (UDR), a framework that reconsiders spacetime not as a fixed geometry but as an emergent system structured by deeper informational principles. His research focuses on rethinking the assumptions underlying physical law, aiming to reconstruct the foundations of gravity, quantum phenomena, and large-scale structure from first principles. He co-founded PrimeSignal Ltd to support scientific development and explore long-term technological applications of his research.
